Put the approved daily brief in YYYY-MM-DD.md, for example 2026-11-16.md.
The date is in Tashkent time. No dated brief means no generation or post.
Use brief-template.txt as a guide; do not queue the template itself.

Optional source images, fonts, audio and product screenshots go in assets/YYYY-MM-DD/.
They will be copied into that day's isolated project under assets/input/.
Use content you are authorized to send to Codex and publish.

Do not put tokens, passwords, .env files or unrelated company documents here.
There is no automatically repeating standing brief until content requirements arrive.
