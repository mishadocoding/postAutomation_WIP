# Circuit Market — promo directions

Source: ../SampleERP/README.txt, index.html, styles.css and features.js.
The product is a local demonstration UI, not a production ERP. Preserve the demo label, use fictitious records, and make no speed, revenue, authentication, accounting or integration claims.

Assumptions: 20–25 seconds, 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, English promo copy, real product screens in English, no voiceover. Music and action sounds are desired; use available bundled sound effects and check catalog authentication before choosing music. No paid media is selected.

## 1. One sale, everything connected — recommended

Device: one product travels through a task. Form: object-to-object chain, returning to the full workspace.
The viewer follows a real sample product from available inventory through a new order and its fulfillment. Enlarged real interface details and a traveling selection outline connect the steps; the completed order returns to its place in the workspace.

First two seconds: the actual inventory product row appears full width; “One sale” lands beside the highlighted quantity.
Beats: available product → create order → stock deduction → Processing / Shipped / Completed → return to workspace → Circuit Market end card.
Look: product white and #f7f9f8 grounds, #27755d green, strong sans-serif titles, clean selection strokes; no tilted UI or invented controls.
Sound: short electronic instrumental with dry clicks on actual actions and one resolved end hit; no voiceover.
Remember: follow a sale and its stock in one workspace.

## 2. From scattered to sorted

Device: before and after. Form: split screen collapses into one stable workspace.
Abstract labels for stock, sales and purchasing arrive out of order, with no implied competing app. The real Circuit Market workspace replaces the scattered composition, then one real order workflow demonstrates the connection.

First two seconds: oversized “Stock?”, “Orders?”, “What changed?” collide on a white field, then snap to the product's navigation rhythm.
Beats: scattered tasks → collapse into real workspace → create one order → see stock change → completed order → end card.
Look: charcoal type on white, abrupt green wipes, the actual product UI on its own paper ground.
Sound: interrupted syncopated opening becomes steady instrumental; clicks follow the cursor.
Remember: bring daily sales operations together.

## 3. The workspace speaks your language — bold alternative

Device: one interface changes language. Form: match cuts hold the same screen geometry while its labels change.
The actual UZ, RU and EN language selector drives three matched views of the same workspace. Kinetic typographic cuts connect those views to a single real inventory-to-order task, ending on the three language codes and the product name.

First two seconds: the real UZ selector fills the frame; its click opens a match cut through RU and EN labels without moving the screen's geometry.
Beats: UZ → RU → EN → choose available product → create order → return to workspace → end card.
Look: real green-and-white UI with bold editorial typography; local font files must support Cyrillic and Uzbek glyphs.
Sound: three distinct selector clicks, beat cuts and an instrumental finish; no voiceover.
Remember: the same daily workflow in Uzbek, Russian and English.
