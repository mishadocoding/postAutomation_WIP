# Why version 1 needs a remake

The user's reread identified mismatched elements, inconsistent geometry, weak animation and a soulless app walkthrough. The prior checks measured runtime and several layout facts; they did not establish art direction or continuity. Do not repeat the claim that passing those checks proves the film is good.

## Concrete findings

1. The source creation button appears approximately at x=733–889, y=1162–1222 in the 1080px composition; the authored click ring starts at x=724, y=1122, 40px above the button's top. It is a decorative ignored element, so the layout audit never tested its contact. The pointer, ring and button were not driven by one shared anchor.
2. Product captures carry different browser focus rings, including a green ring around the close control in some stills but not others. The crossfade therefore changes both the business value and unrelated decoration, creating an unmotivated state jump.
3. Several screenshot windows crop the source at arbitrary heights. Hiding the overflow satisfied intent annotations but did not make the crop an intentional shot or preserve a shape through transitions.
4. Large diagonal rectangles have no relationship to the product's task or to objects in adjacent scenes. They decorate the canvas without explaining or causing anything.
5. Full-product screenshots reduce interface text below a useful phone reading size. Large editorial callouts rescue the words, but the pictures still feel like a generic presentation of software.
6. The main structure is a chain of titles and screenshots. There is no protagonist, physical situation, surprise, visual payoff or consistent carrier object.
7. Sparse clicks and whooshes cannot supply the emotional structure the user now wants. Compose to actual music before timing the remake.

## Remake review gates

- Establish an art bible: one contour weight, perspective, corner treatment, palette, font hierarchy and scale relationship for all illustrated objects.
- Establish a stable prop identity: one receipt, token or torn poster persists through the film, with explicit handoff positions and layer order.
- Give each action anticipation, contact, follow-through and a readable reaction. Move connected props through shared anchors; never animate their coordinates independently.
- Put the actual intended attention grab in the first second; never label a slow screenshot entrance “explosive.”
- Audit a contact sheet containing start, contact, release and settle for every hero action, plus the normal beat, seam and phone sheets.
- Inspect unrelated changes between paired before/after proof captures; remove focus rings, caret states and other capture artifacts consistently in a new isolated capture, never by inventing a product control.
- Keep technical checks, but also write P0/P1 notes on drawing quality, physical contact, purposeful layer order, continuity and the visual joke. Do not silence a geometry mismatch with a broad ignore attribute.
- Music is required for the remake. Auth or a user-provided music file is still pending.
